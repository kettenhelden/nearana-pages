2. How Nearana works

Nearana is an independent iOS client for Audiobookshelf. The app connects directly over HTTPS to the Audiobookshelf server you choose. Normal playback and sign-in connect directly to your Audiobookshelf server. Chapter notifications run locally on your iPhone, or through a dedicated push service for a separately connected private server (see section 4a).

3. Sign-in and credentials

Your server address, username and password are sent to your chosen Audiobookshelf server for authentication. The server provides access and refresh tokens. Nearana saves the server address and tokens in the protected iOS Keychain, but does not retain your typed password as a permanent app password. Signing out removes stored account tokens.

4. Media and playback progress

Nearana retrieves media metadata, covers, chapters, streams and listening progress from the connected server. Playback positions and session information are synchronized back to that server. Covers are temporarily cached in memory and iOS may create temporary playback/network buffers. Version 1.1 does not offer complete offline audiobook downloads.

Local app settings and playback checkpoints may include server address, book ID, position, timestamps, playback speed and skip durations. Optional bookmarks containing the audiobook ID, chapter title, listening position and timestamp are stored only on this iPhone and scoped to the connected Audiobookshelf server and signed-in user; they are not synchronized through the Nearana push service. Confirmed new chapters may be marked locally until you open the audiobook or the marker expires. Your Audiobookshelf server operator is responsible for data stored or logged on that server.

4a. Optional chapter notifications (Nearana 1.1)

Chapter notifications are optional and disabled by default. For most Audiobookshelf servers, Nearana checks for updated chapters directly on your iPhone when opened and during background refreshes permitted by iOS. The sign-in tokens and saved chapter baselines needed for this remain on your device. The app can show local notifications for new chapters. iOS decides whether and when background refresh runs, so prompt delivery cannot be guaranteed.

For an independently configured, owner-operated private Audiobookshelf server, Nearana can instead use a dedicated push service at push.kettenhelden.com. After opt-in, a short-lived Audiobookshelf access token is transmitted once over an encrypted connection to verify your existing account access; it is not stored permanently. Passwords and refresh tokens are not sent to this service. A random device identifier, encrypted Apple push token, push subscription identifier and the necessary permitted library identifiers are stored. An already configured chapter watcher transmits audiobook and chapter metadata such as titles, chapter offsets and durations when changes occur, but not audio files. Messages are delivered through Apple Push Notification service (APNs).

You can turn notifications off in Nearana at any time. For private push, this stops delivery to your own device without interrupting monitoring for other authorized devices. Signing out revokes your device's subscription when the service is reachable; a shared owner-operated watcher remains in place. Contact the address above for access or deletion requests. Processing serves the optional notification feature only, not advertising. For users in the EEA, the legal basis for this opt-in is Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

5. Public demo

The app can connect to the shared third-party Audiobookshelf demo at audiobooks.dev. Technical connection data, including IP address, are received by the demo operator. Other users of the shared demo account can affect listening progress. Do not use it for private content.

6. Analytics and tracking

No first-party advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs were identified in the currently reviewed Nearana code. This does not cover processing independently performed by your Audiobookshelf server or Apple. These statements should be rechecked when the app changes.

7. This website

Our support website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub processes technical connection data such as IP address and requests for operational and security reasons. The website has no first-party tracking scripts, advertising cookies or externally hosted fonts. For details, see GitHub's privacy statement. The legal basis for providing this website is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

8. Support emails

If you contact us, we process the contact details and information you provide to respond. Depending on the request, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. We retain requests only as long as needed or legally required. Do not email passwords or access tokens.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability where applicable, and object to certain processing. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. For data stored on your own Audiobookshelf server, contact its operator.

We will update this policy when the app's processing or legal requirements change.
