2. How Nearana works

Nearana is an independent iOS client for Audiobookshelf. The app connects directly over HTTPS to the Audiobookshelf server you choose. Normal playback and sign-in connect directly to your Audiobookshelf server. If you opt in to chapter notifications, Nearana additionally uses a dedicated push service that processes the necessary book metadata (see section 4a).

3. Sign-in and credentials

Your server address, username and password are sent to your chosen Audiobookshelf server for authentication. The server provides access and refresh tokens. Nearana saves the server address and tokens in the protected iOS Keychain, but does not retain your typed password as a permanent app password. Signing out removes stored account tokens.

4. Media and playback progress

Nearana retrieves media metadata, covers, chapters, streams and listening progress from the connected server. Playback positions and session information are synchronized back to that server. Covers are temporarily cached in memory and iOS may create temporary playback/network buffers. Version 1.0 does not offer complete offline audiobook downloads.

Local app settings and playback checkpoints may include server address, book ID, position, timestamps, playback speed and skip durations. Your Audiobookshelf server operator is responsible for data stored or logged on that server.

4a. Optional chapter notifications (Nearana 1.1)

If you explicitly enable chapter notifications, Nearana may use a separate push service at push.kettenhelden.com. It processes a randomly generated device identifier, an Apple-provided push token, a random subscription identifier and necessary connection information. A separately configured chapter watcher on the corresponding Audiobookshelf host sends the book identifiers and titles, chapter names, durations, start times and pseudonymous audio-file fingerprints needed to detect updates. No audiobook files, Audiobookshelf passwords or Audiobookshelf API keys are transmitted to the push service. Device push tokens are stored encrypted.

When a new chapter is detected, the service sends the information needed for the notification through Apple Push Notification service (APNs) to your device. Book and chapter titles may appear in the notification. This feature is optional, requires a user opt-in and also requires an operational chapter watcher on the server. You can disable notifications in settings at any time. Further alerts stop once your choice has been synchronized with the service. When you sign out, Nearana attempts to delete your server subscription and its associated chapter records. If the service is unreachable, deletion may need to be retried or requested using the contact address above. This information is processed to provide and troubleshoot the optional push service, not for advertising. For users in the European Economic Area, the legal basis for opting in is Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

5. Public demo

The app can connect to the shared third-party Audiobookshelf demo at audiobooks.dev. Technical connection data, including IP address, are received by the demo operator. Other users of the shared demo account can affect listening progress. Do not use it for private content.

6. Analytics and tracking

No first-party advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs were identified in the currently reviewed Nearana code. This does not cover processing independently performed by your Audiobookshelf server or Apple. These statements should be rechecked when the app changes.

7. This website

Our support website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub processes technical connection data such as IP address and requests for operational and security reasons. The website has no first-party tracking scripts, advertising cookies or externally hosted fonts. For details, see GitHub's privacy statement. The legal basis for providing this website is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

8. Support emails

If you contact us, we process the contact details and information you provide to respond. Depending on the request, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. We retain requests only as long as needed or legally required. Do not email passwords or access tokens.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability where applicable, and object to certain processing. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. For data stored on your own Audiobookshelf server, contact its operator.

We will update this policy when the app's processing or legal requirements change.
