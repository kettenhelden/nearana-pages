Help with setting up, connecting and using Nearana with Audiobookshelf.

Frequently asked questions

How do I connect to Audiobookshelf?

Enter the full HTTPS address of your reachable Audiobookshelf server, along with your Audiobookshelf username and password. Nearana connects directly to this server.

Do I need my own server?

To listen to your personal library, you need access to a compatible Audiobookshelf server. You can explore the app with the built-in public demo.

Can I listen offline?

Not in version 1.0. Nearana streams audiobooks and currently does not offer offline downloads.

Playback does not start. What should I do?

Check your internet connection, HTTPS server address and server availability. Try the same audiobook in the Audiobookshelf web player. For support, provide your iOS and Nearana versions, audio file format and the error message. Never send passwords or access tokens.

How is progress synchronized?

Nearana reads progress from your connected Audiobookshelf server and sends updated listening positions to it. Public demo users share progress with other visitors.

Is CarPlay supported?

Nearana supports CarPlay audio on compatible iPhones and vehicles. Check your device and vehicle settings if it does not appear.

How do I sign out?

You can sign out in Nearana's settings. Saved account tokens are deleted locally, but the account and content on your Audiobookshelf server remain.
